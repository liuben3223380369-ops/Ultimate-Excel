公式引擎：基于 HyperFormula v3.4，开源业界标准（与 Excel/WPS 兼容），支持 400+ 函数，包括：
数学/三角：SUM, PRODUCT, AVERAGE, MIN, MAX, COUNT, MEDIAN, STDEV, ABS, SQRT, POWER, MOD, ROUND, FLOOR, CEILING, INT, LOG, LN, EXP, SIN, COS, TAN, ASIN, ACOS, ATAN, ATAN2, PI, RAND, RANDBETWEEN, SUMSQ, SUMPRODUCT, FACT, COMBIN, GCD, LCM, SIGN
统计：MEDIAN, MODE, VAR, STDEVP, VARP, LARGE, SMALL, PERCENTILE, QUARTILE, RANK, PERCENTRANK, TRIMMEAN, SKEW, KURT, GEOMEAN, HARMEAN, COVAR, CORREL, SLOPE, INTERCEPT
文本：CONCAT, CONCATENATE, LEFT, RIGHT, MID, LEN, FIND, SEARCH, SUBSTITUTE, REPLACE, LOWER, UPPER, PROPER, TRIM, REPT, TEXT, VALUE, EXACT, CHAR, CODE, NUMBERVALUE, UNICODE, T, FIXED
日期：TODAY, NOW, DATE, YEAR, MONTH, DAY, WEEKDAY, HOUR, MINUTE, SECOND, DAYS, EDATE, EOMONTH, WORKDAY, NETWORKDAYS, DATEDIF, YEARFRAC, WEEKNUM, ISOWEEKNUM, DATEVALUE, TIMEVALUE
逻辑：IF, IFS, IFERROR, IFNA, AND, OR, NOT, SWITCH, XOR, TRUE, FALSE, ISBLANK, ISNUMBER, ISTEXT, ISERROR, ISNA, ISLOGICAL, ISREF, ISNONTEXT, ISFORMULA
查找：VLOOKUP, HLOOKUP, LOOKUP, XLOOKUP, INDEX, MATCH, XMATCH, OFFSET, INDIRECT, ROW, COLUMN, ROWS, COLUMNS, ADDRESS, HYPERLINK, CHOOSE, AREAS
财务：PV, FV, PMT, NPV, IRR, RATE, NPER, EFFECT, NOMINAL, PPMT, IPMT, CUMIPMT, CUMPRINC, SLN, DB, DDB, SYD, TBILLYIELD, DURATION, MDURATION
信息：TYPE, ERROR.TYPE, NA, N, CELL, INFO, ISPMT, ACCRINT, COUPNUM
数组/动态：FILTER, SORT, SORTBY, UNIQUE, SEQUENCE, RANDARRAY, LET, LAMBDA（部分已支持）
文件兼容：
· 导入：.xlsx / .xls / .csv / .json
· 导出：.xlsx（保公式、样式、合并）/ .csv / .json
快捷键：Ctrl+Z/Y 撤销重做 · Ctrl+C/X/V 复制剪切粘贴（与 Excel 互通）· Ctrl+B/I/U 字体格式 · Ctrl+F 查找替换 · Ctrl+S 另存为 xlsx · Delete 清除 · F2 编辑 · Ctrl+A 全选 · Ctrl+滚轮 缩放
操作技巧：拖动行列边界调整宽高；拖动选区右下角绿色方块自动填充（数字等差+公式引用自动偏移）；双击工作表标签重命名；右键单元格打开完整菜单；图表支持拖拽移动。
